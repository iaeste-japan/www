This page explains the amount to be paid to IAESTE Japan, as well as the travel and accommodation fees required for the internship.

## Amount to Be Paid to IAESTE Japan

You need to pay the application fee and the program participation fee. Please see below for details.

### Application Fee

| Classification | Student of University Member (*1) | Student of Non-Member School (*1) |
| -------------------- | --------: | ---------: |
| First Time Applicant | JPY 5,000 | JPY 10,000 |
| Applicant with previous IAESTE internship experience | 0 JPY | 0 JPY | 

### Program Participation Fee

Half of the program participation fee will be refunded if the internship is done remotely.

| Classification | Student of University Member (*1) | Student of Non-Member School (*1) |
| -----------------| ---------: | ----------: |
| First Time Applicant | JPY 50,000 | JPY 100,000 |
| Applicant with previous IAESTE internship experience | 25,000 JPY | 50,000 JPY | 

1. See [here (Content in Japanese)](/org/members/) for current university member schools.<br>Even if your university is a non-member school at the time of application, the amount for university member students will be applied in the following cases.

   - If your university becomes a member school by the time of the internship.
   - If the candidate's affiliation changes to a university member school by the time of the internship. (e.g., admission to a master's program)

## Other Expenses 

| Item | Contents |
| ------------------ | ------------------ |
| Travel Expenses | You are to purchase your airline tickets and other expenses. |
| Accommodation | The internship host usually provides sufficient funds for your living expenses. You may be required to pay for your own expenses to participate in local IAESTE-sponsored events. |
| Overseas Accident Insurance | You are responsible for your own insurance, including arrangements. Insurance is mandatory. |
| VISA Application Fee | You will be responsible for this fee. Assistance to VISA acquisition will be provided. |
