We accept September applications for internship students from `September 1st (Mon) to September 20th (Sat)`.

Applicants must be `at least a third-year undergraduate student` and `enrolled in a Japanese university or graduate school` at the time of the internship.

For details, please refer to the `Appliction Guidelines (for September)` at the following page.
- [Application Guidelines and Required Documents](../required-docs.md)

Application documents are updated periodically. <br>
Please make sure to use the latest version when submitting your application.
