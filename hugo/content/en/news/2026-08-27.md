title: "September recruitment of internship students"

IAESTE Japan will accept applications for internships from Tuesday, September 1, to Thursday, September 17, 5:00 p.m. (JST)
Please apply through the application portal, which will be open during this period.<br>
Applicants must be enrolled at a Japanese university or graduate school and be in their third year of undergraduate study or above at the time of the internship.<br>

The application procedure and required documents have been revised. Please review the latest instructions on the [Application Process & Documents](../required-docs.md) page before submitting your application.<br>
Please note that some of the minimum English proficiency score requirements have been revised.
