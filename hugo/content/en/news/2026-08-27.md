IAESTE Japan will accept applications for internships in September as follows.

- **Application period**: Tuesday, September 1 – Thursday, September 17, 5:00 p.m. (JST)
- **Eligibility**: Applicants must be enrolled at a Japanese university or graduate school and be in their third year of undergraduate study or above at the time of the internship.

Please apply through the application portal, which will be open during this period.

The application procedure and required documents have been revised. Please review the latest instructions on the [Application Process & Documents](../required-docs.md) page before submitting your application.

Please note that some of the minimum English proficiency score requirements have been revised.
