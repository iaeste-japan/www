title: "April recruitment of internship students and updates in application method."
---
 
We accept April applications for internship students from `April 1st (wed) to April 24th (Fri)`.
 
Please note that the only offer type available for the April recruitment is the `Global Internship`, which differs from the `Exchange Internship` in that it requires a higher English test score and has a different training availability period.

<!--more-->

Applicants must be `at least a third-year undergraduate student at the start of internship` and `enrolled in a Japanese IAESTE member university or graduate school till the end of the internship` as well.

For details, please refer to the `Application Guidelines (for April)` at the following page.

- [Application Guidelines and Required Documents](../required-docs.md)

In addition, the application method will change from submitting documents by email to online registration using a designated form. Please note that there are no changes to the application requirements or required documents.

The Application Guidelines and required documents on the above pages will be updated on April 1st (planned). Therefore, applicants are asked to follow the revised versions when completing the application procedure.
