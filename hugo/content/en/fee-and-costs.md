This page explains the amount to be paid to IAESTE Japan, as well as the travel and accommodation fees required for the internship.

## Amount to Be Paid to IAESTE Japan

You need to pay the application fee and the internship administration fee. Please see below for details.<br>
This applies to applications submitted on or after September 1, 2026.

### Application Fee

| Classification | Student of University Member (*1) | Student of Non-Member School (*1) |
| -------------------- | --------: | ---------: |
| First Time Applicant | JPY 6,000 | JPY 12,000 |
| Applicant with previous IAESTE internship experience | JPY 0 | JPY 0 | 

### Internship Administration Fee

| Classification | Student of University Member (*1) | Student of Non-Member School (*1) |
| -----------------| ---------: | ----------: |
| First Time Applicant | JPY 60,000 | JPY 120,000 |
| Applicant with previous IAESTE internship experience | JPY 42,000 | JPY 84,000 | 

1. See [here (Content in Japanese)](/org/members/) for current university member schools.<br>If you belong to a university member school at either the time of application or the time of internship, the amount for university member students will be applied.

## Other Expenses 

| Item | Contents |
| ------------------ | ------------------ |
| Travel Expenses | You are to purchase your airline tickets and other expenses. |
| Accommodation | The internship host usually provides sufficient funds for your living expenses. You may be required to pay for your own expenses to participate in local IAESTE-sponsored events. |
| Overseas Accident Insurance | You are responsible for your own insurance, including arrangements. Insurance is mandatory. |
| VISA Application Fee | You will be responsible for this fee. The host country's IAESTE will assist you with the VISA application process. |
