This page explains the process from applying for a position at IAESTE Japan to starting your internship.

## Glossary

The table below explains the terms used within this page.

| Item | Content |
| ---------------------- | ----- |
| **Internship Host**  | A company or university that offers internship opportunities to students. |
| **Offer** | A predetermined format in which the internship host states the student's field of expertise and required skills to be accepted by the host institution, as well as the accommodation and housing to be provided |
| **Intern Candidate**  | After application, the student must complete an interview with the professor in charge of IAESTE Japan and be approved as an Intern Candidate. The right as a candidate is valid as long as you have a student registration. |
| **IAESTE A.s.b.l.** | A non-profit organization registered in Luxembourg and the international umbrella organization for IAESTE. A.s.b.l. stands for `Association sans but lucratif`.|
| **Exchange Platform** | A system for opening and submitting offers operated by IAESTE A.s.b.l.. You need to register first to use the system. |

## Steps to internship

Application is accepted twice a year; it is common to apply in September and have an internship during the summer of the following year.

Yet, the timing and duration of the internship will depend on the conditions of the offer.

| Application Period | Offers Applicable |
| ------ | ------- |
| **September Recruitment** | Exchange Internship and Global Internship |
| **April Recruitment** | Global Internship only |

For details on Exchange Internship and Global Internship, please refer to [What is offer?](offers.md).

Please refer to [Application Process and Required Documents](required-docs.md) for information on application.

### September Recruitment

The following table explains the process to start an internship if you apply in September.

| Monthly Schedule  | Exchange Internship | Global Internship |
| -------- | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| **September** | Submit application form and other necessary documents | Same as on the left |
| **October** | Applicants must pass an interview with the professor in charge of IAESTE to be an `Intern Candidate'. | Same as on the left |
| **November<br>~<br>February** | IAESTE Japan exchanges offers with other member countries based on each candidate's specialization and preferences, and assigns them to each candidate (called "matching")　| The following steps will be repeated until the internship host is confirmed. <ol><li>Students: Select a preferred offer from the Exchange Platform and contact the Japan Office with the Reference No. to be assigned</li><li>Students: After being assigned, prepare the nomination documents and email them to the Japan Office </li><li>Internship Host : final review</li><li>The Japan Office: forward the review results to the student</li></ol>On confirmation of Internship Host, for a country that requires VISA, apply and obtain it. |
| **From<br>March<br>onwards** | The candidate submits Nomination Documents, which are then reviewed by the Internship host. <br>↓After receiving the review result notification, attend the internship preparation, and complete procedures for VISA etc. | Continue the above procedure until the Internship Host is confirmed. |
