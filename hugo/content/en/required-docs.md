## About Entry

> [!CAUTION]
> 1. There are two types of internships: Exchange Internship and Global Internship. For the differences, please refer to [What is Offer?](offers.md).
> 2. Please note that the available internship periods and types of offers differ between the September recruitment and the April recruitment.
> 3. You must submit proof of your English proficiency, including your test scores, within the application period. Please take an English test early enough to obtain your scores before the application deadline.
> 4. Please see the [Contact](contact.md) page for our office hours. During our office hours, we are available to respond to inquiries about matters not covered in the documents on this page.

## Minimum English Proficiency Requirements
When applying, you will be asked to select an offer type (see [What is Offer?](offers.md)). The minimum English test score required depends on the offer type you select. The score must be no more than two years old when the nomination documents are submitted.

| Examination | Exchange Internship | Global Internship |
| --- | :-----: | :-----: |
| **TOEFL iBT** (from Jan. 2026) | 4 | 4.5 |
| **TOEFL iBT** (before Jan. 2026) | 72(*1) | 86(*1) |
| **IELTS** | 5.5 | 6.5 |
| **Cambridge English Qualifications** | B2 First | C1 Advanced |
| **STEP Eiken** | Grade Pre-1 | Grade Pre-1 |
| **TOEIC Listening & Reading** | 650 | 785 | 

*1: Because the TOEFL iBT score reporting system changed in January 2026, applicants may use TOEFL iBT scores obtained before the change for the time being. However, as some internship hosts may require the new TOEFL iBT score, we strongly recommend obtaining a new score before your Nomination Documents are submitted.

### Notes on English Proficiency after Application

1. Depending on the host country, proof of your Speaking and/or Writing proficiency may also be required. If you only have a TOEIC Listening and Reading score, you may need to demonstrate these skills through an additional interview or take another English test. Our office will contact you if this applies to your application.
2. After you submit your application, the host institute may interview you to assess your suitability for the internship before making its acceptance decision. You should therefore be prepared to demonstrate your practical English communication skills.
